About Kashif

I'm a Senior Product Designer with 10+ years designing SaaS, Healthcare, and Fintech products. I don't just improve how things look. I remove the friction that blocks adoption and growth.

My focus is simple: find the hidden UX problems that slow your product down, and fix them. For founders and product leads, that means turning complex systems into interfaces that ship faster and perform better.

My Operating Principle: ROI-First Design

I don't just design interfaces. I design user pathways that serve your business goals. My process is built on one non-negotiable premise: you should see a clear return on your design investment.
  • Metrics-Driven Diagnosis: I audit your funnel and pinpoint where users drop off.
  • Hypothesis-Led Execution: Every design decision supports a clear narrative that guides, informs, and converts.
  • Systematic Scalability: I build design systems that ensure consistency. Not one-off art projects.
  • Validation & Iteration: My job isn't done at launch. I measure results against original goals.

Problem Statement

EcoCharge — Green Tech Mobile Platform

Mobile App • Energy • Consumer

Problem Statement

The EV charging landscape is fragmented, drivers had "charger anxiety" because locators were unreliable, payments were confusing, and there was no real-time information. A seamless new solution was required to capture the expanding market

What I Did

  • Mapped out a smooth navigation experience with real-time route optimization.
  • Created a unified Find → Charge → Pay flow with seamless UX for easy payment choices.
  • Focused the solution on linking users to green charging spots to align with the platform's core mission.

Impact

Find → Charge → Pay
One unified flow with real-time route optimization
Simple Payments
Clear payment choices, no confusing steps
Green Charging Focus
Designed around green charging spots, aligned to platform mission

Talent Conversations — AI Recruitment Platform

AI • HRMS • SaaS

Problem Statement

Recruiters were spending excessive time manually reviewing, sorting, and evaluating candidates. The existing ATS lacked automation, leading to slow screening cycles and inconsistent evaluations.

What I Did

  • Designed conversational AI flows so recruiters can ask questions and instantly surface candidate summaries
  • Built a candidate comparison interface with side-by-side profiles and deep-dive cards
  • Created evaluation dashboards enabling inline ratings, comments, and workflow optimization.

Impact
