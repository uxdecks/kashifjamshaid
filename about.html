About Kashif

I'm a designer and developer with 15+ years building digital products across SaaS, healthcare, fintech, and real estate. I now support agencies who sell that work to their clients.

I now work as a white-label design and frontend partner for agencies. When your team is booked and a client says yes, I take the brief, handle the design and frontend execution under your brand, and hand it back on time. Your client relationship stays yours. I stay invisible.

My range covers product UI, SaaS dashboards, web app interfaces, web design, frontend, and pitch decks. That means you can send me product and web briefs and I will produce them under your brand.

How I work with agencies:

  • White-label by default. Your brand on everything. My name nowhere.
  • Direct communication. You talk to the person doing the work.
  • Fast turnaround. 24-48h on small requests.
  • No onboarding overhead. Send a brief. Get the work back. Move on.
  • One partner, multiple outputs. Product UI, web apps, frontend, decks, design systems.
